# Assignment Transfer Review

1. Identify the source and receiving parties, agreement/lease, affected tract or interest, effective date, and supplied instrument.
2. Compare the assignment scope and fraction with the underlying lease and ownership records.
3. Check for required signatures, authority, exhibits, consents, notices, and recording evidence only when configured or supplied.
4. Preserve conflicts between effective date, execution date, and recording date.
5. Produce a proposed record update and a requirements list; never update a registry or conclude that title transferred.
6. Route missing instruments, jurisdictional uncertainty, and payment-impacting changes to human review.
