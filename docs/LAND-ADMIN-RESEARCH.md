## Supported operating recipes

1. Maintain lease, contract, ownership, and obligation records as structured
   business data with source documents attached.
2. Review ownership and interest changes before producing a division order or
   payment-impacting recommendation.
3. Track expirations, rentals, development commitments, assignments, and other
   deadlines before they become defaults or lost acreage.
4. Research title, assignments, and federal or jurisdictional records while
   preserving provenance and unresolved defects.
5. Route legal interpretation, conflicting ownership evidence, and external
   communication to qualified human reviewers.

## Research-derived expansion

The SAIT curriculum groups the work around title/ownership, mineral lease
documentation (including rentals, caveats, transfers, and assignments), lease
record keeping, joint-venture agreements, and surface land. PETEX separately
calls out title chaining/curing, lease interpretation and interest calculations,
and oil-and-gas law. Quorum's operational guidance emphasizes one connected
record of agreements, ownership, documents, obligations, deadlines, approvals,
and audit history. These patterns motivated the expanded lease lifecycle,
title-chain, and division-order assets in this repository.

The simple division-order formula used in the fixture is supported by PennEnergy:
`(acres in unit / total unit acres) * royalty rate`. Real cases may involve
partial tracts, allocation factors, carved-out interests, amendments, or other
terms, so the agent must preserve inputs and route exceptions rather than treat
the formula as universal.

These recipes lead to the agents, skills, and flows in this domain. They do not
authorize the system to certify title, approve payments, file instruments, or
contact owners.
